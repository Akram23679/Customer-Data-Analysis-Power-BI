Gender · Q1–Q3

Women make 6 of every 10 purchases, buy 6 of every 10 items and bring 6 of every 10 lira.

Answer: Female customers lead on all three measures (59.8%). Their average order value is the same as men's (688 vs 691 TL), so women do not spend more per visit; there are simply more female shoppers.

Answer: Clothing has 34.7% of purchases and 45.3% of revenue. Clothing, Shoes and Technology together make 94.8% of revenue. Food & Beverage is 14.9% of purchases but only 0.3% of revenue, because items cost 5.23 TL. Every category is about 60% female, and every age group buys the same mix, so category choice does not depend on gender or age.
